## 1. Executive Summary

This audit reviewed the semantic, economic, and operational claims across FinSight's documentation, SQL analytics, model reports, and codebase to eliminate misleading real-world inferences, clarify synthetic simulator limitations, and ensure defensible financial terminology.

### Key Corrections & Clarifications:
1. **Simulation Step $\neq$ Real-World Timestamp:**
   `step` is an integer clock cycle ($1\text{--}743$) in an agent-based discrete-event simulator representing 1-hour increments. It does **not** map to physical calendar dates, time zones, daylight savings, or real bank holidays.
2. **Simulated Fraud Exposure $\neq$ Realized Financial Loss:**
   The reported **$12,056,415,427.84** ($\approx \$12.06\text{B}$) across 8,213 fraud-labelled transactions represents **gross fraud-associated transaction volume (simulated fraud exposure)**, not confirmed, unrecoverable chargeback loss:
   - In real-world payment networks, gross transaction exposure undergoes chargeback recovery, card network re-presentment, funds clawback, pre-settlement authorization holds, and merchant liability allocation.
   - Conflating gross exposure with net write-off loss overstates financial loss by orders of magnitude.
3. **Synthetic Simulator Constraints:**
   PaySim simulates fraud exclusively through automated agents executing paired extractions: funds are transferred (`TRANSFER`) to a drop account and immediately withdrawn (`CASH_OUT`). No fraud exists on `PAYMENT`, `CASH_IN`, or `DEBIT` rails. Real-world fraud typologies (authorised push payment fraud, merchant bust-out, identity theft, synthetic identities, credit card skimming) are absent from the dataset.
4. **Prototype Scope & Production Disclaimers:**
   FinSight is an educational, portfolio-grade analytical prototype. It has not undergone demographic parity audits, fair lending reviews (ECOA/FCRA), or real-time low-latency stress testing, and is strictly **not production-ready**.

## 2. Terminology & Semantic Mapping

| Term in Legacy Reports | Audited & Corrected Terminology | Semantic & Forensic Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Financial Fraud Loss"** | **Gross Fraud-Associated Transaction Volume** / **Simulated Fraud Exposure** | A $10,000,000 payment that is flagged and blocked, or recovered via interbank freeze, does not result in $10M of realized loss. Terminology must reflect exposure, not net P&L write-off. |
| **"Calendar Timestamp"** / **"Date"** | **Simulation Step** / **Simulated Elapsed Day** | `step` is a discrete 1-hour counter in PaySim. Days 1–31 are ordinal simulation days, not actual calendar dates. |
| **"Confirmed Fraud"** | **Simulated Fraud Label** (`isFraud = 1`) | Labels in PaySim are generated by programmatic simulator rules, not forensic law-enforcement convictions. |
| **"Fraud Probability"** | **Uncalibrated Model Score** | Model outputs under balanced class weights are monotonic risk indices ($\in [0, 1]$), not empirical posterior probabilities. |
| **"Safe / Auto-Approve"** | **Low Risk Tier (Standard Automated Queue)** | A model score below threshold does not guarantee legitimacy; it indicates that the transaction did not trigger risk monitoring gates. |
| **"Fraudulent Event" (re: `type_PAYMENT = 0`)** | **Absence of Non-Fraud Rail Suppression** | In one-hot encoding, a zero simply reflects the rail used; it is not an independent fraudulent act. |


### Rail Exposure Distribution:
- **`TRANSFER` Fraud Volume:** $4,116$ transactions totalling **$6,028,208,443.43** (49.999% of fraud exposure).
- **`CASH_OUT` Fraud Volume:** $4,097$ transactions totalling **$6,028,206,984.41** (50.001% of fraud exposure).
- **Other Rails (`PAYMENT`, `CASH_IN`, `DEBIT`):** $0.00$ fraud exposure ($0.00\%$).

**Forensic Observation on Paired Simulators:**
In PaySim, fraud amounts on `TRANSFER` and `CASH_OUT` are virtually identical in count and aggregate dollars because the simulator's agent scripts execute synchronized two-step extractions: Agent A transfers $\$X$ to Agent B via `TRANSFER`, and Agent B immediately withdraws $\$X$ via `CASH_OUT`. Counting both transactions as separate cumulative "losses" double-counts the same underlying funds. The true simulated economic leakage is $\approx \$6.03\text{B}$, not $\$12.06\text{B}$.

## 4. Production Readiness & Regulatory Disclaimers

Project documentation, README, and executive reports must prominently feature the following governance notice:

> [!CAUTION]
> **Educational & Portfolio Prototype Notice:**
> FinSight is a retrospective data engineering and machine learning analytics prototype developed for portfolio and technical demonstration. 
> 
> **Key Operational Guardrails:**
> 1. **Synthetic Data Boundaries:** All evaluations are conducted on synthetic data generated by the PaySim agent-based simulator. Performance metrics on PaySim cannot be assumed to transfer to live banking networks.
> 2. **No Inline Authorization:** FinSight operates as a batch analytical intelligence platform and is not designed for sub-100ms real-time payment authorization.
> 3. **Fairness & Compliance:** The model has not undergone disparate impact analysis, protected-class fairness testing (e.g. under US ECOA / Regulation B or EU GDPR Article 22), or model governance model-risk validation (SR 11-7).
> 4. **Human Review Requirement:** Model alerts serve exclusively as investigative cues for human compliance analysts. Automatic adverse action (e.g. account closure, credit denial) without human review is strictly prohibited.
